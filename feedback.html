Nutrition & Healthy Eating Directory

Explore nutrition programmes, cooking initiatives, and healthy eating opportunities linked to SDG 3.

Student Cook-Along — Campus Kitchen Club

A free weekly cooking session teaching students how to make quick, nutritious meals on a budget. No experience needed — all ingredients are provided.

Join Now

Community Food Bank — Nutrition Volunteer

Help sort and distribute healthy food packages to low-income families in the local area. Volunteers commit to 2 hours per week. Full training is provided.

Get Involved

Eat Well Live Well — Campus Awareness Campaign

A student-led initiative promoting the NHS Eatwell Guide on campus. Includes pop-up information stalls, free recipe cards, and a smoothie-making workshop.

Learn More

Batch Cook & Budget — Weekly Meal Prep Workshop

Learn how to plan and prep a full week of balanced meals for under £20. Covers food labels, portion sizes, and reducing food waste. Held every Sunday afternoon.
